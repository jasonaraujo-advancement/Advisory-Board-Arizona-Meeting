What to Pack

The Day 2 visit to Mt. Hopkins runs from the afternoon into the night. Temperatures at the summit are much cooler than in Tucson after sunset — please plan accordingly.

For the Mountain

  • Warm jacket, plus a hat and gloves for after sunset
  • Layers you can add or remove through the afternoon and evening
  • Comfortable, closed-toe walking shoes with good grip
  • Reusable water bottle — staying hydrated helps at altitude

For Tucson

  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat
  • Business attire for the Advisory Board meetings
  • Comfortable shoes for the Mirror Lab tour

Star Party Tips

  • Bright screens and white flashlights affect night vision — please dim your phone and follow staff guidance on lighting
  • Let us know in advance if you have any concerns about time at high elevation
