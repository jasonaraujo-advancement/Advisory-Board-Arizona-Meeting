Trip Details

Trip Details

April 15 – 18, 2027  ·  Tucson, Arizona

Arrival & Departure

Please plan to arrive at the Omni Tucson National Resort on Thursday, April 15 in time for the 6:00 pm Welcome Dinner. Departure is on Sunday, April 18 at any time.

Ground Transportation

A coach bus will take the group from the Omni to the Fred Lawrence Whipple Observatory on Friday and return in the evening. On Mt. Hopkins, guests will transfer to observatory vehicles for travel to the Ridge and the MMT. On Saturday, the group departs the Omni at 9:00 am for the University of Arizona, and the bus returns guests to the Omni after dinner at the Arizona Inn.

Meals

  • Thursday: Welcome Dinner at Bob's Steak & Chophouse
  • Friday: Breakfast on your own · boxed lunch on the bus · dinner at the Whipple Dormitory · hot cocoa at the star party
  • Saturday: Breakfast on your own · lunch at the University of Arizona · reception and dinner at the Arizona Inn
  • Sunday: Breakfast on your own

Meetings

Working Committee Meetings — Friday, April 16, 10:00 – 11:30 am at the Omni.
SAO Advisory Board Meeting — Saturday, April 17, beginning at 1:00 pm at the University of Arizona.
