SAO in Arizona

SAO Advisory Board Meeting & SNAC Excursion

April 15 – 18, 2027  ·  Tucson, Arizona

Members of the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory Advisory Board will gather in Tucson, Arizona from April 15 – 18, 2027 for the spring Board Meeting and an excursion to SAO's premier observing site in the American Southwest.

Over four days, guests will hold working committee meetings and the full Board Meeting, spend an afternoon and evening on Mt. Hopkins at the Fred Lawrence Whipple Observatory — including the VERITAS array, the Ridge telescopes, and a sunset visit and star party at the MMT — and tour the University of Arizona's Richard F. Caris Mirror Lab, where the world's largest telescope mirrors are cast and polished.

Trip at a Glance

Thursday | April 15 — Arrival at the Omni Tucson National Resort and Welcome Dinner.

Friday | April 16 — Committee meetings at the Omni, then the Whipple Observatory, dinner on the mountain, and a star party at the MMT.

Saturday | April 17 — Mirror Lab tour in the morning, Board Meeting in the afternoon, and reception and dinner at the Arizona Inn.

Sunday | April 18 — Departure.
