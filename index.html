Desert to Cosmos

Arizona Advisory Board Meeting & Excursion

Join this exclusive gathering of Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory Advisory Board Members, SNAC Members and Smithsonian leadership and scientists on this unforgettable tour of the Whipple Observatory, University of Arizona Mirror Lab, and other astronomy-focused attractions.

The Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory Advisory Board and the Smithsonian National Astronomy Council (SNAC) Champion members are cordially invited to Tucson, Arizona to join our Director Lisa Kewley and Deputy Director Michael McCarthy on an immersive experience to learn about the cutting-edge technologies developed by the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory in Arizona.

Few places on Earth offer such extraordinary access to the tools and minds advancing our understanding of the universe. Set amid the dramatic landscapes of the Sonoran Desert, this special gathering combines the Advisory Board meeting with exclusive visits to some of the world's most important astronomical facilities.

Program Highlights

  • Exclusive visit to the Fred Lawrence Whipple Observatory
  • Sunset and evening experience atop Mt. Hopkins
  • Private access to the MMT Observatory
  • Stargazing under some of the darkest skies in the continental United States
  • Behind-the-scenes tour of the Richard F. Caris Mirror Lab at the University of Arizona
  • Discussions with researchers and observatory staff
  • Meetings and receptions set against the backdrop of the Sonoran Desert
From the Desert to the Cosmos

Over four days, journey from giant mirrors to mountaintop observatories

Participants will journey from the giant mirrors being crafted for the next generation of telescopes to mountaintop observatories peering deep into the cosmos. Along the way, we will engage with leading scientists, explore pioneering research facilities, and enjoy memorable dining experiences beneath Arizona's famed dark skies.
